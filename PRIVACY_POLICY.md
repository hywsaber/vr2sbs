# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

`vr2sbs` collects **0 user data**.

- No vr2sbs account is created, and none is required; the optional Bilibili sign-in is handled by Bilibili's own website inside an embedded browser
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No advertising identifiers
- No collection, storage, or sale of personal information by the developer

If you open a video URL, the app connects only to that URL to play the video you requested. Any data handling by third-party websites, Google Play, or your device OS is governed by their own policies.

If you choose to remember an SMB account, its server, share, domain, and username are stored in the app's private device storage. The password is encrypted with a non-exportable key in Android Keystore. These credentials are used only to connect directly from your device to the SMB server you select; they are not sent to the developer.

Signing in to Bilibili is optional. The sign-in page is Bilibili's own website, displayed in an embedded browser; your username and password are entered there and are not stored by vr2sbs. Bilibili may set session cookies on your device, which vr2sbs keeps in the app's private WebView cookie store. Those cookies are sent only to Bilibili when requesting video information and streams for the link you open. They are not sent to the developer. Use **Switch Bilibili account** to replace the session, or clear the app's data to remove it.

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository.
